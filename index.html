FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How long does it take to complete a project?

Standard delivery is usually 24-48 hours. For more complex projects like high-end 3D montages or full channel branding, it might take up to 3-5 days. I always prioritize quality over speed!

What payment methods do you accept?

I accept local bank transfers (Sri Lanka), Binance (Crypto), and PayPal for international clients. Payment is usually 50% upfront and 50% after completion.

Do you offer revisions if I'm not satisfied?

Absolutely! I offer unlimited revisions for the Gold package and up to 2 revisions for the Basic package. My goal is to make sure you are 100% happy with the final result.

Which software do you use for your work?

I mainly use Adobe Premiere Pro for editing, After Effects for VFX/Motion Graphics, Photoshop for Thumbnails/Graphics, and Blender for 3D designs.
